```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Start: Intend to Plasma Clean Samples (approx. 30 seconds)] --> B(Open Plasma Cleaner Chamber Door);

    B --> C{Is Tray Removal/Adjustment Needed?};
    C -- Yes --> D[Retrieve/Adjust Tray (e.g., for cleaning/positioning)];
    C -- No --> E[Prepare Sample Box & Tweezers];

    D --> E;
    E --> F(Open Sample Box);

    F --> G{Load Wafer into Chamber};
    G --> H[Grasp Wafer with Tweezers from Sample Box];
    H --> I[Place Wafer on Tray inside Chamber];
    I --> J[Remove Tweezers from Chamber];
    J --> K{More Wafers to Load?};
    K -- Yes --> H;
    K -- No --> L[Close Sample Box & Set Down Tweezers];

    L --> M(Close Plasma Cleaner Chamber Door);
    M --> N[Power On Plasma Cleaner & Activate Vacuum/Plasma];
    N --> O[Set RF Level to HIGH];
    O --> P{Operate Valves & Adjust Pressure};
    P --> Q[Monitor Pressure (e.g., "stop moving knob once pressure reaches")];
    Q -- Pressure OK --> R[Plasma Cleaning in Progress];
    Q -- Adjust Further --> P;

    R --> S[Shut Down Plasma Cleaner];
    S --> T[Turn RF Level to OFF];
    T --> U(Open Plasma Cleaner Chamber Door);

    U --> V{Unload Wafer from Chamber};
    V --> W[Grasp Cleaned Wafer with Tweezers from Cleaner];
    W --> X[Place Wafer into Sample Box];
    X --> Y[Remove Tweezers from Box];
    Y --> Z{More Cleaned Wafers to Unload?};
    Z -- Yes --> W;
    Z -- No --> AA[Close Sample Box & Set Down Tools];

    AA --> BB[End];
```
